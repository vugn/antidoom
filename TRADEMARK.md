that license. All rights reserved by vugn.

What this means in practice:

- You can fork, study, change and ship this code. That is what the license is for.
- If you publish your fork (on Google Play, F-Droid or anywhere else), give it
  a different name and icon, and change the `applicationId`, so nobody mistakes
  it for the official AntiDoom.
- Saying your app is "based on AntiDoom" or linking back here is fine and welcome.
- Unmodified builds from this repository, packaged by distributors such as
  F-Droid, may keep the name and icon.
